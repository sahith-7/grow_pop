Store details and GST billing

Only the owner can change these. Check them with your accountant before your first real sale: they are printed on every invoice.

Store details

In Settings, enter your business's legal name, address with state and PIN code, phone and email. The state matters: it decides whether a sale is charged CGST and SGST or IGST.

Billing and GST

In Billing, choose how you are registered:

  • Regular: enter your GSTIN. Sales get a tax invoice; if every item in a sale is 0% GST, a bill of supply.
  • Composition: sales get a bill of supply, without tax shown.
  • Not registered: sales get a simple bill.

Prices you enter for products include GST, as on your shelf labels. The software works out the taxable value and tax so that the total always equals the shelf price.

Invoice numbers

Each financial year (April to March) gets its own series, for example INV/26-27/1 for invoices and CN/26-27/1 for credit notes. Numbers are never skipped or reused, even when two counters bill at once. Ask your accountant before the first invoice of a year if you need a different format.

GST rates

Each product has a GST rate and an HSN code. If you are unsure of a rate, ask your accountant; the software applies what you enter.
