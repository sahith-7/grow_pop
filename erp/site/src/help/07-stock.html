Stock, stocktakes and purchasing

Every change to stock is a recorded movement: who, when, why, and the count after it. Nothing changes stock silently, so you can always see how a number came about in Stock movements.

Locations

Your store starts with two locations, "Shop floor" and "Back room". Add others, such as a godown. Each location has its own stock.

Opening stock

When you start, enter the stock you have of each item at each location as opening stock.

Adjustments

Correct a count, write off damaged or expired goods, or move items to damaged stock, always with a reason. The owner can allow one adjustment to go below zero, with a written reason; everyone else can't.

Transfers

Move stock from one location to another. It leaves one and arrives in the other in the same step.

Stocktakes

  1. Start a stocktake for a location. Counters see the items but not the expected numbers, so they count what is really there.
  2. Enter the counts. Items not counted are left unchanged.
  3. The owner or a manager reviews the differences and approves. Sales made during the count are allowed for.

Buying from suppliers

  1. Add your suppliers.
  2. Make a purchase order with the items, quantities and costs.
  3. When goods arrive, receive them against the order. Record any damaged on arrival: they go to damaged stock, not to the shelf. You can't receive more than is still outstanding on an order line; extras go on a separate line.
  4. Return faulty goods to the supplier from the receipt.

Receiving updates each item's average cost, which the inventory reports use.
