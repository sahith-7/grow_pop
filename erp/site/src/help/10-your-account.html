Yearly payment costs less. See pricing.

Paying

  1. On the Account page, note the amount, our UPI ID and your store code (6 letters and numbers).
  2. Pay from any UPI app. Put the store code in the payment note, so we can match the payment to your store.
  3. Once we record it, your plan is active and a receipt appears on the Account page with the payment history.

Paying early never loses days: the new period starts when the current one ends.

If payment is late

For 7 days after the end date, everything works and a banner reminds you. After that the store is read-only: everyone can see everything, but no changes or sales can be made until payment is recorded. Web orders still arrive and wait. You can always sign in, see the Account page and download your data.

Download all your data

In Account, Data export, ask for an export. A ZIP file with one spreadsheet (CSV) per kind of record is ready in a few minutes and can be downloaded for 7 days. Passwords and shop keys are left out. Each download is recorded in your audit log. You can make up to 3 exports a day.

Support access

Our team can't see inside your store unless you allow it. If you ask for help, switch on support access in Account for 1 to 72 hours and say why. Each look we take is recorded in your audit log, and you can end access at any time. No one from our side can sign in as you or your staff.

Your team

In Team, invite the people who work with you and give each a role:

  • Owner: everything, including billing, voids and the Account page.
  • Manager: day-to-day running, approvals, reports.
  • Cashier: the counter and returns.
  • Inventory: products, stock and purchasing.
  • Fulfilment: picking, packing and dispatching web orders.

Your plan sets how many people can have access. When someone leaves, remove them: they are signed out straight away.
