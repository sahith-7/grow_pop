Selling at the counter

Make a sale

  1. Open the counter (POS) screen. It works full-screen on a computer or tablet.
  2. Scan each item, or search by name or SKU.
  3. Add the customer's phone number if you want them on the invoice or to find the sale later. It is optional.
  4. Take payment: cash, UPI or card.
  5. Finish the sale. The invoice number is given at this moment, and the invoice is ready to print.

Discounts

You can give a discount on a line or on the whole bill. Each role has a limit set by the owner; a cashier can't go above it, and above a set percentage the software asks for a reason. The owner has no limit.

Printing

Print on an 80 mm receipt printer or on A4 from the browser's print window. You can reprint any invoice later from Invoices.

When an item is out of stock

A sale can't take stock that the software says isn't there, or that is held for a paid web order. If the item is really on the shelf, the stock count is wrong: correct it with a stock adjustment, then sell. This keeps your website from selling the same piece.

Today's takings

The counter shows today's takings by payment method, with refunds taken off, so you can check your cash drawer at closing.
