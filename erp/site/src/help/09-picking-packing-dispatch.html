Picking, packing and dispatching web orders

Owners, managers and fulfilment staff can do this.

Pick

  1. In Fulfilment, orders that are paid and have all their stock held are ready to pick.
  2. Make a pick list of up to 30 orders. An order line can be on only one active list.
  3. Open the list on a phone or tablet and walk the shelves. Mark each item as picked.

If an item is missing or damaged, take the order off the list: its stock stays held for it, and you can deal with it later. There are no substitutions: what is packed must match the order.

Pack and dispatch

  1. Pack each order. Every line must be fully picked.
  2. Dispatch: enter the courier name, and the service and tracking number if there is one (hand delivery and local couriers don't need one).

On dispatch, the stock leaves your shop, the GST invoice for the order is made, and your website is told: the customer gets a note with the courier and tracking number, and the order is marked completed, which sends WooCommerce's "order complete" email.

If the customer changes the order

If the order is cancelled or refunded on the website while you pick, it comes off the list and its stock is released. If items or quantities change, it comes off the list and the new items are held. The screen tells you which orders changed.

One parcel per order: split deliveries, courier bookings and shipping labels are not available yet.
