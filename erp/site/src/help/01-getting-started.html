Getting started

Sign up

Go to the sign-up page and enter your shop's name, your name, your email and a password. The person who signs up becomes the store's owner, with full control. You can add staff later.

Your free trial

Your store starts with a {{trialDays}}-day free trial with everything switched on, including the WooCommerce connection. You will get a reminder 7 days and 1 day before it ends. Your Account page always shows how many days are left.

The "Getting started" list

Your dashboard shows a short checklist. Each step links straight to the right screen, and ticks itself once done:

  1. Store details: your business name, address and contact details.
  2. Billing and GST: your registration type, GSTIN and invoice settings.
  3. Products: add a few by hand or import your list.
  4. Barcodes: give every item a barcode and print labels.
  5. Online shop (Store + Online plan): connect your WooCommerce website.
  6. Staff: invite the people who work with you.
  7. First sale: make a test sale at the counter.

You can hide the list when you no longer need it.

What you need

A computer or tablet with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. A USB barcode scanner and a receipt or label printer help but are optional: any scanner that types the code like a keyboard works.
