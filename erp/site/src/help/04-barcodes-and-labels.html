Barcodes and labels

Items that already have a barcode

Scan the barcode into the product's barcode field. Most USB scanners type the number like a keyboard and press Enter, so they work without any setup.

Items without a barcode

Let the software create one. It makes in-store barcodes that can't clash with makers' barcodes, and it never gives the same number twice.

Printing labels

Choose the products and how many copies of each, then pick a label size:

  • 50 × 25 mm or 38 × 25 mm thermal labels (one label per page on a label printer)
  • A4 sheets with 65 labels (38.1 × 21.2 mm)

Labels show the barcode, name, SKU, price and MRP. They print from your browser: in the print window, turn off headers and footers and set the scale to 100%. Print one test label and scan it before printing a whole batch.
