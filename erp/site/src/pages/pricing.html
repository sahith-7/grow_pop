How paying works

  1. Your Account page shows our UPI ID, the amount, and your store's 6-character code.
  2. Pay from any UPI app and put the store code in the payment note.
  3. We match the payment and your plan is active, usually the same working day. Your receipt appears on the Account page.

Paying early never loses days: the new period starts when the current one ends. You get reminders 7 days and 1 day before your trial or paid period ends.

If a payment is late

  • For the first 7 days after the end date, everything keeps working and a banner reminds you.
  • After that, the store becomes read-only: you and your staff can see everything but can't make changes or new sales until you pay. Web orders still arrive and wait for you.
  • You can always download all your data from the Account page, even when read-only or closed.

Questions about price, or a store with several branches? Talk to us. Refunds are covered in the refund policy.
