Features

Everything below works today. The list at the end says what it does not do yet, so you can decide with the full picture.

At the counter

  • Fast billing by barcode scan or search, on a computer or a tablet.
  • GST worked out on the server: CGST and SGST for counter sales, with prices that include GST. Tax invoice, bill of supply or invoice-cum-bill of supply, depending on your registration and what you sell.
  • Line and bill discounts, with limits per role and a reason above the limit you set.
  • Cash, UPI and card payments recorded with each sale.
  • Print on A4 or an 80 mm receipt printer from the browser.
  • Returns against the original invoice with a credit note, part returns included. The owner can void an invoice.
  • A sale can't take stock that isn't there, or stock already held for a web order.

Products and barcodes

  • Products with sizes, colours and other options, each with its own SKU, price, MRP and barcode.
  • Use the maker's barcode, or let {{productName}} create one for items that have none.
  • Print barcode labels on 50 × 25 mm or 38 × 25 mm thermal labels, or A4 sheets of 65.
  • Import your product list from a spreadsheet (CSV), with a preview of every row before anything is saved.
  • Product photos, categories and brands.

Stock and buying

  • A complete stock ledger: every movement says who, when and why.
  • Several locations (shop floor, back room, godown) and transfers between them.
  • Adjustments with reasons, damaged stock kept apart from sellable stock.
  • Stocktakes counted without showing the expected number, approved by the owner or a manager.
  • Suppliers, purchase orders, goods received (with damaged-on-arrival), and returns to supplier.

Your WooCommerce shop

  • Connect your WooCommerce website with its API keys. Your existing products are matched by SKU before anything changes.
  • Name, description, price, stock, category and photos are sent from {{productName}} to the website for products you mark "sell online".
  • Paid web orders arrive on their own and hold their stock; stock leaves only when the order is dispatched.
  • Cancelled or refunded orders release their stock. A regular check-up catches anything a webhook missed.

Packing web orders

  • Pick lists of ready orders, with a phone-friendly picking screen.
  • Pack and dispatch with the courier name and an optional tracking number; your customer gets it by email from WooCommerce.
  • The GST invoice for a web order is made when it is dispatched.

Reports

Sales, inventory summary, low stock, stock movements, stocktake variance, purchasing and online sync health, each downloadable as CSV. A daily low-stock email goes to the owner and managers.

People and safety

  • Roles for owner, manager, cashier, inventory and fulfilment staff. Each sees only what the role allows.
  • An audit log of every important change.
  • Download all your data as spreadsheets whenever you want, even if you stop paying.
  • Our support team can see inside your store only after the owner switches on access, for up to 72 hours, and every look is in your audit log.
  • Your data is kept in India (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai), encrypted, and backed up every night.
