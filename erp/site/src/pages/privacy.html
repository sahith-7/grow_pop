This policy explains how {{legalName}} ("we") handles personal data in {{productName}} and on this website, under the Information Technology Act, 2000, its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Two kinds of personal data, two roles

  • Data about you and your staff (names, email addresses, phone numbers, sign-in records): we decide why and how this is used, so for it we are the data fiduciary.
  • Data about your customers that your store enters or receives from your website (names, phone numbers, addresses, what they bought): your store decides why it is collected, and we process it only on your store's behalf and on its instructions, to provide the service. Your store is responsible for telling its customers about it and for having a lawful reason to collect it.

2. What we collect about you

  • Account details: business name, your name, email, phone, and your staff's names and emails.
  • Business details you enter: legal name, GSTIN, address.
  • Payment records: amount, date and the UPI or bank reference of payments to us. We do not see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank login.
  • Security records: sign-ins, the network address used, and an audit log of changes in your store.
  • Messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.

This website itself sets no cookies, has no tracking or advertising code, and stores nothing you type in the demo form; that form only opens WhatsApp or email on your device.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: sign-in, your store's records, invoices, emails the service sends (reminders, alerts, password resets).
  • To bill you and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To keep the service secure: detecting misuse, investigating incidents.
  • To support you when you ask, and, for your customers' data, only with support access the owner switches on.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping tax records and reporting security incidents to CERT-In.

We do not sell personal data and do not use your store's data for advertising.

4. Where it is kept and who helps us

Your data is stored in India, in Amazon Web Services' Mumbai region, encrypted at rest and in transit. We use these service providers, each bound to protect it:

  • Amazon Web Services (hosting, database, file storage, backups, sending email), India
  • Cloudflare (website delivery and protection against attacks; it sees network traffic but does not store your store's records)
  • The email provider we use for our own mailbox

We will update this list before adding a provider that handles your store's data.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your store is active, for as long as you keep it in the service.
  • After a store is closed: 30 days so you can download it, then deleted, except records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices and payment records for the period required by GST law (at least 72 months from the due date of the annual return), and security logs for 180 days.
  • Nightly backups are kept up to 35 days (monthly copies up to 12 months) and then deleted automatically.

6. Security

Stores are kept apart in the database itself, so one store cannot see another's data. Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes. Our own staff access is limited, protected by a second sign-in factor and logged. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without delay and report it as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can see and correct your account details in the service, download all your store's data, and ask us to delete your account. You can also ask us what personal data about you we hold, and nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. Write to our grievance officer below. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are a customer of a store that uses {{productName}}, please contact that store first; we will help it answer you.

The service is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.
